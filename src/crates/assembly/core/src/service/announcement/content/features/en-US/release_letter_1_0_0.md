# To every friend who supports OpenBitFun

OpenBitFun 1.0.0 is here.

Every star, every piece of feedback, and every contribution has helped us get here. Thank you.

From Rust Agent Runtime and Agent Harness to the GUI, we have kept refining every detail with one wish in mind: to help more people build what they have always wanted to create.

1.0.0 brings four Agent Harness options, each fully capable in its own right. Choose the one that best fits the task at hand. From computers to phones, watches, and browsers—and eventually glasses—we want OpenBitFun to feel closer to your everyday life. Whenever you want to make something, it will be within reach, helping you turn an idea, step by step, into something real.

We will stay true to the spirit of open source and keep making OpenBitFun more reliable and easier to use. In more people's hands, we hope it will become something we cannot yet imagine.

**Create with it. And help create what comes next.**
